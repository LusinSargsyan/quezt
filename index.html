FAQ

Everything you need to know.

What is Quezt?

A no-code gamification platform. You add missions, badges, levels, XP and leaderboards to your product as a branded widget.

Who is it for?

Any digital product that wants players to come back: iGaming, e-commerce, SaaS, edtech. Growth, product and developer teams each get their own tools.

Do I need a developer?

To try it, no: paste your URL and see your widget. To go live, a developer adds one snippet and sends events from your server (or connects Shopify).

Can players get cash?

No. Quezt gives XP, badges and coupons you issue. Never cash or bonus money.

What happens to my data if I go over the limit?

Nothing is locked. You keep full access and export; we alert you at 80% and 100%.

How does the pilot work?

Free and invite-only for 4 launch partners. We help you set up your first campaign. Spots and dates: TBD.
